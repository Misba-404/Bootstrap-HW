Frequently asked Qns

Which board is best for a headless Linux environment?
The Raspberry Pi 5 is the recommended choice for running full Linux distributions (like Ubuntu, Kali, or DietPi) in a headless configuration, offering the necessary RAM and processing architecture.
Can I easily connect standard sensors to the ESP32?
Yes. The ESP32 features multiple GPIO pins that easily interface with temperature, motion, and proximity sensors, making it an excellent choice for lightweight robotics projects.
What are the power requirements for these boards?
Microcontrollers like the ESP32 and Arduino are highly power-efficient and can run off simple battery packs or USB. The Raspberry Pi requires a dedicated 5V/5A power supply for optimal, continuous high-load operations.
